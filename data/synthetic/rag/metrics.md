## Metric definition

A processed request is a request marked complete within the sample reporting period. Reopened requests are counted once, at their final completion. Both sample periods use this same definition. No customer names, credentials, private support tickets or real company identifiers are included in this fixture.

## Data quality

The source deliberately does not contain a causal explanation. Future analytics should distinguish supplied facts from hypotheses. These sentences also make the fixture long enough to exercise a bounded chunk with an overlap while retaining paragraph locations.
